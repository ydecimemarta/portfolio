Play Without Labels

Breaking gender stereotypes through play

Research UX Design Visuals

Gender roles still quietly shape the worlds children imagine for themselves.

Motivated by a shared vision, we formed a voluntary collective of designers, educators, and developers in 2022 to design a game that introduces kids aged 3–6 to professions, fostering creativity, skills, and equality.

Pepupo is where play meets possibility, opening doors to a future free from stereotypes.

The problem

Parents want to free their kids from the gender roles they grew up with

Many parents grew up with messages pushing girls toward beauty and service, and boys toward power and leadership, leaving little room for vulnerability or care.

Now, they want something different for their kids. Yet, children today still absorb subtle gender stereotypes from media, toys, and daily life. Breaking these patterns starts with awareness and the right tools early on.
